Safety Rules

How ClearLine classifies content
Hate Speech

Attacks targeting a person or group

Content that demeans, attacks, or calls for discrimination against individuals based on identity, race, religion, or origin.

"Go back where you came from" "People like you are a disease"
Threat

Content implying harm or violence

Any message that directly or indirectly threatens physical harm, stalking, or violence against a person.

"Watch your back" "I know where you live"
Offensive

Insulting or derogatory language

Hostile, demeaning, or abusive language intended to insult or humiliate without a specific threat or hate speech element.

"You're such an idiot" "Shut up you moron"
Safe

No harmful content detected

Neutral, positive, or constructive content that does not contain any threatening, hateful, or offensive language.

"Great job today!" "Hope you have a great day"
Model Info

TF-IDF + Logistic Regression · 82.6% accuracy

Trained on 47,692 real social media posts. Text is preprocessed by removing URLs, mentions, and special characters before classification.
